# Pavoria Sentinel

**An AI-powered fraud detection and forensic intelligence framework for the voluntary carbon market.**

*Currently in stealth development. A working prototype is operational on Google Earth Engine, analyzing live satellite data against real-world carbon project claims.*

---

## The Problem

The voluntary carbon market is projected to grow from approximately $4 billion today to over $1 trillion by 2050. It is simultaneously one of the most opaque, under-policed, and structurally fraud-prone markets in modern finance.

Independent academic studies have suggested that a substantial portion of credits issued by leading certifiers may not represent genuine, additional, or permanent emissions reductions. Mechanisms of fraud include synthetic baselines, ghost projects, double-counting, beneficial-ownership obfuscation, and trade-based money laundering through carbon credit issuance and resale.

The infrastructure to detect these patterns at scale — combining geospatial verification, financial forensics, statistical anomaly analysis, and entity-network intelligence in a single defensible signal — has not previously existed.

Pavoria Sentinel is being built to fill that gap.

## The Framework

Pavoria Sentinel applies a four-layer verification framework. Each layer draws on a distinct, mature discipline. The integration of all four into a single risk signal is the novel contribution.

### 🛰️ 1. Geospatial Intelligence
Satellite-based verification of vegetation cover, deforestation rates, canopy height, and land-cover change, evaluated against the project's reported claims. Built on remote sensing methodologies and global Earth observation data sources.

### 🔍 2. Forensic Financial Intelligence
Transaction-level monitoring of credit issuance, retirement, and resale, applying AML, sanctions, and counterparty-risk methodologies adapted from global banking. Detects trade-based money laundering, layering, and credit-flow patterns inconsistent with legitimate project economics.

### 📊 3. Mathematical Anomaly Detection
Statistical and pattern-based analysis to surface baseline manipulation, reporting irregularities, and quantitative red flags that are not visible through manual review. Draws on established forensic accounting and statistical fraud detection techniques.

### 🕸️ 4. Entity & Network Intelligence
Beneficial ownership mapping, adverse media screening, jurisdictional risk scoring, and relationship-graph analysis across project developers, intermediaries, validators, and end buyers.

Together, these four layers produce a single, defensible risk score for any carbon project — designed for banks, investors, registries, regulators, auditors, and corporate buyers.

## Scope

The framework is methodology-agnostic across project types — including land-based forestry (REDD+, ARR), blue carbon (mangroves, seagrass, salt marshes, tidal wetlands), soil carbon, and emerging credit categories. Each project type carries distinct fraud signatures, and the framework is designed to accommodate them.
